SGCS — Sistema de Gestão de Chamados para Síndicos

Objective
Organize how condominiums log, classify, and track service requests.
My contribution
I managed the project, coordinated the team, tracked deadlines and deliverables, and integrated the tools we used.
Outcome
Our team at Fatec Franco da Rocha developed a functional prototype and received an honorable mention at VI SIMBAJU in 2026. The SaaS product is in development.
