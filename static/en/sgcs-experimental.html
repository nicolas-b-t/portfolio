SGCS — Sistema de Gestão de Chamados para Síndicos

Our team at Fatec Franco da Rocha developed a prototype to help professional condominium managers log, classify, and track service requests. The SaaS product is in development.

The project considers the sector’s professionalization and potential requirements of future regulation, which is still under discussion in Brazil’s National Congress.

I managed the project, coordinated the team, and tracked deadlines and deliverables. I also administered and integrated the tools we used.

The project received an honorable mention at VI SIMBAJU – Energia e Tecnologia in 2026. Read the published chapter in Portuguese.

Team credited at VI SIMBAJU
