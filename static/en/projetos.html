Academic research · SaaS in development

SGCS — Sistema de Gestão de Chamados para Síndicos

Objective
Give professional condominium managers a simple, accessible way to log, classify, and track service requests.
My contribution
I managed the project, coordinated the team, and tracked the schedule and deliverables. I administered and integrated the project tools.
Outcome
Our team at Fatec Franco da Rocha developed a working prototype for condominium service request management. The project received an honorable mention at VI SIMBAJU in 2026. The software-as-a-service (SaaS) product is in development.
Keywords
  • Condominium management
  • Service request management
  • Low-code prototyping

View the SGCS case study

Award-winning project team at VI SIMBAJU

Ismael Jackson Barbosa Silveira, Rikelme Fernandes Lopes, Miller de Souza Reis, Nicolas Beraldes Tarifa, and Rodrigo do Nascimento Kussano Carneiro.

The project considers the sector’s professionalization and potential requirements of future regulation, which is still under discussion in Brazil’s National Congress.
